# Pictaria

Have an idea to build an app that does puzzles there's already plenty of puzzle apps out there they use a grid that has rectangular tiles you can do 3x3 4x4 5x5 and 6x6 and that's the level of difficulties this particular app would be something that people could load their own pictures into and create like a temp page story book of say the baby's first birthday or their trip to Hawaii or if you're a zoo on my monthly tier then you might do all of the primates one day or 10 pictures of the same group of primates it doesn't matter but basically it gives the social media agencies something fun to do to get more engagement and also it gives the end user human a way to celebrate by creating puzzles of their favorite pictures and sending them out.... I like puzzle games where there's a slight sound and vibration when a puzzle piece fits into the other puzzle piece they lock in they can't unlock and you can move them around and hook them to other pieces...
